Know Your Vaccine

Explore the illustrated timeline of vaccines in the Indian immunization schedule

BCG

Description: Protects against tuberculosis (TB).

Dosage: Single dose at birth.

Method: Intradermal injection.

Side Effects: Localized skin reaction, mild fever.

OPV

Description: Provides immunity against poliovirus.

Dosage: Multiple doses: birth, 6, 10, 14 weeks.

Method: Oral drops.

Side Effects: Rare cases of vaccine-derived polio.

Hepatitis B

Description: Prevents Hepatitis B infection.

Dosage: Birth, 6, 10, 14 weeks.

Method: Intramuscular injection.

Side Effects: Pain at injection site, mild fever.

MMR

Description: Protects against measles, mumps, rubella.

Dosage: Two doses: 9-12 months, 16-24 months.

Method: Subcutaneous injection.

Side Effects: Rash, fever, joint pain (rubella).

Anti-Rabies

Description: Prevents rabies infection after exposure.

Dosage: 4 doses on days 0, 3, 7, and 14 after exposure.

Method: Intramuscular injection.

Side Effects: Pain at injection site, mild fever, headache.

COVID-19

Description: Protects against SARS-CoV-2 virus.

Dosage: Typically 2 doses, with boosters as recommended.

Method: Intramuscular injection.

Side Effects: Fatigue, headache, muscle pain, fever (usually mild).

Influenza (Flu)

Description: Protects against seasonal influenza viruses.

Dosage: Annual vaccination recommended.

Method: Intramuscular injection or nasal spray.

Side Effects: Soreness at injection site, low-grade fever.

Typhoid

Description: Prevents typhoid fever.

Dosage: Single dose, with boosters every 3 years if needed.

Method: Intramuscular injection or oral capsules.

Side Effects: Fever, headache, nausea (usually mild).

Hepatitis A

Description: Protects against Hepatitis A virus infection.

Dosage: Two doses, 6 months apart.

Method: Intramuscular injection.

Side Effects: Soreness at injection site, mild headache.

For more information, consult your healthcare provider.
